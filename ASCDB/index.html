A Small(er) Chemistry Database

A statistically significant database for the evaluation and parametrization of electronic-structure methods, built from the three largest computational-chemistry databases (GMTKN55, MGCDB84 and Minnesota 2015B). Pick a group to inspect its reactions and reference energies, explore every molecule in 3D, and download the data.

ASCDB is a distilled version of the ACCDB collection, where the full parent databases can be browsed.
